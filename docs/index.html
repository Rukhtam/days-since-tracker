HOW IT WORKS

Simple & Intuitive

Get started in seconds. No learning curve required.

1

Add Your First Tracker

Tap the + button and either choose from 35+ templates or create a custom tracker. Set your name, interval, icon, and color.

2

Watch Progress Visually

Your progress ring fills up as days pass. Green means you're on track, yellow warns you're approaching the interval, red alerts when overdue.

3

Get Smart Reminders

Receive notifications at 90% of your interval. Enable/disable per item or globally. Never miss an important recurring task again.

4

Reset with One Tap

Completed the task? Tap the reset button. The counter resets to day 0 and your progress ring starts fresh. Undo available if you tap by mistake.
