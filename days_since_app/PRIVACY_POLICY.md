# Privacy Policy for Days Since Tracker

**Last Updated:** January 6, 2026

## Overview

Days Since Tracker ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how the App handles your data.

## Data Collection

**The App does NOT collect, transmit, or share any personal data.**

All information you enter into the App is stored **locally on your device only**. No data is sent to external servers or third parties.

## Data Stored Locally

The App stores the following information on your device:
- Tracker items you create (names, intervals, reset dates, icons, colors)
- App settings and preferences (theme, notification settings, sort order)
- Notification schedules

This data is stored using local storage (Hive database) and never leaves your device.

## Permissions

The App requests the following permissions:

### Notifications Permission (Android 13+)
- **Purpose:** To send reminder notifications when your tracked items are approaching their due dates
- **Required:** Optional - the App functions fully without notifications
- **Control:** You can enable/disable notifications at any time in the App settings

### Device Boot Permission
- **Purpose:** To restore scheduled notifications after device restart
- **Required:** Optional - only needed if you enable notifications

### Vibration Permission
- **Purpose:** To provide haptic feedback for user interactions
- **Required:** Optional - enhances user experience but not essential

## Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device:
- Your data is as secure as your device
- No data is transmitted over the internet
- No account or login is required
- Uninstalling the App will permanently delete all local data

## Third-Party Services

The App does **NOT** use any third-party services, analytics, or tracking tools.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Data Deletion

To delete all App data:
1. Go to your device Settings
2. Navigate to Apps → Days Since Tracker
3. Tap "Clear Data" or "Storage" → "Clear Storage"

OR

Simply uninstall the App to remove all data permanently.
