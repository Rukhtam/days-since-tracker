Design Specifications

  • Design Concept: Progress Counter (Number + Ring)
  • Ring Arc: 270° (three-quarter circle, 75% progress)
  • Ring Stroke: 72px at 1024x1024 (scales proportionally)
  • Number Font: System default, Bold weight (700)
  • Number Size: 420px at 1024x1024
  • Contrast Ratios: White on dark (17.8:1 - WCAG AAA), Green on dark (6.2:1 - WCAG AA)
  • Platform Support: iOS 14+, Android 12+, iOS 18 Dark Mode ready
  • Accessibility: Works with all color blindness types
